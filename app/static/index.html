Quanteinstein Value Proposition

"Quanteinstein is a risk-guarded quantitative strategy incubation and paper-trading sandbox that bridges unstructured market intelligence into deterministic algorithmic execution. Through an integrated modular suite—featuring an AI Quant Coach, visual Strategy Builder, historical Backtester, live Charting, Trade Journal, and execution Analytics—it allows traders to formulate mathematical parameters, stress-test rules against historical friction, and forward-incubate strategies in a 100% paper-simulated environment before routing orders to live exchange pipes."

Target Audience & ICP

Systematic retail traders, proprietary trading desks, emerging hedge funds, boutique quant firms, and portfolio management teams—including newly launched firms within their first 6 months—who want to build, backtest, and incubate automated trading strategies without the risk of emotional execution, code-heavy infrastructure overhead, or capital blowups.

Strict Intent Scoring Rules (1-10)

Score 9 - 10

They explicitly mentioned a pain point we solve in a post/comment (slippage, execution friction, backtester divergence, emotional overrides), or their company is actively hiring to solve this exact problem right now.

Score 8

They frequently post about our niche and use the specific tech stack our project requires (Python, pandas/numpy, VectorBT, Backtrader, CCXT, IBKR API, order books).

Score 4 - 7

Right industry/title (Quant PM, Hedge Fund Trader), but no active signals or recent relevant activity. (PASS)

Score 1 - 3

Irrelevant domain (e.g. general marketing, real estate, generic web development). (PASS)

Local Setup & Direct Execution Guide

✉️ 1. Connect Personal Email ID (Gmail / Outlook)

Send real cold outreach directly from your personal inbox through local SMTP:

  1. For Gmail: Turn on 2-Step Verification on your Google Account.
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords and generate a 16-character App Password.
  3. Open .env in your folder and fill in: 
    SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_USER=your_email@gmail.com
SMTP_PASSWORD=xxxx xxxx xxxx xxxx
  4. Verify in CMD: python main.py test-smtp

🔗 2. Connect Your LinkedIn Account

Avoid account ban risks from automated scraper bots. Send connection requests 100% safely:

  1. Ensure you are logged into linkedin.com in your default web browser.
  2. When any prospect scores 8 or higher, click "Connect on LinkedIn".
  3. The engine copies the exact ≤ 45-word hook to your Windows clipboard and opens the prospect's profile directly.
  4. Click Connect → Add a note → press Ctrl + V → Send (takes 3 seconds!).
